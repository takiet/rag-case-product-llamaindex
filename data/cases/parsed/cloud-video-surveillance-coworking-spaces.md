# Scalable cloud video surveillance for innovative coworking spaces

Organization:
Infinite Area

Location:
Montebelluna, Italy

Customer need:
Remote monitoring,
Safety,
Security,
People flow and occupancy

Montebelluna, Italy,
2026

Infinite Area chose Axis products and solutions for a cloud-based video surveillance system achieving proactive protection of people and property, remote management, and seamless integration. The system combines high-quality network cameras, advanced analytics, scalability, and aesthetic harmony, enhancing security and occupancy monitoring across co working spaces and a boutique hotel.

---

## Infinite Area – an AI-first innovation platform

Founded a decade ago as an innovation platform, Infinite Area has evolved into an AI first ecosystem that helps companies adopt artificial intelligence strategically and develop tailored digital solutions. Its mission is to make business processes more efficient, resilient, and ready for future challenges.

The heart of this ecosystem is the Infinite Area Hub in Montebelluna, Italy. This revitalized industrial warehouse has been transformed into a contemporary campus, complete with coworking spaces, event venues, and iconic environments. It's a place where design, technology, and human-centered innovation come together.

![A modern conference room with a futuristic design, featuring a large digital screen displaying "WHERE PEOPLE SHAPE THE FUTURE," an airplane fuselage-like structure with space images, colorful chairs, and large windows with a view of greenery.](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2026-03/cloud%20storage%20security.jpg.webp?h=173e7b15&itok=zsjCbfd0)

## From on-site servers to cloud storage security

At the Infinite Area Hub, the aim was to eliminate on-site server storage and enable complete remote management. Addtionally, to leverage network cameras as smart sensors for [occupancy and space utilization.](https://www.axis.com/solutions/occupancy-and-space-utilization)

The goal was to build a next-generation, cloud-based video surveillance infrastructure that supports proactive protection, remote management, and seamless architectural integration.

“Video surveillance was created to protect places and people, but we also consider it a fundamental tool for monitoring attendance patterns and ensuring that resources intervene when and where action is required,” explains Patrizio Bof, President and founder of Infinite Group. “With Axis, we found a partner capable of guaranteeing long‑term continuity and building solutions in a ‘building block’ mode. This approach allows us to achieve lasting results and a more effective return on investment.”

## The cloud-based video surveillance solution

Axis collaborated with Infinite Area to deploy a fully [cloud-based solution](https://www.axis.com/solutions/cloud-connect) that combines advanced analytics, operational agility, and aesthetic harmony within one of Italy’s most distinctive innovation hubs.

To meet Infinite Area’s requirements, Axis partnered with Eurotel, an Axis partner, to design a fully cloud‑based architecture powered by [AXIS Camera Station Edge](https://www.axis.com/products/axis-camera-station-edge). This approach eliminates the need for on‑site servers, storing recordings directly on the cameras via high‑reliability SD cards and enabling complete remote management.

“One of the added values of our consulting services comes from the strength of the Axis channel program, of which Eurotel is a Solution Gold Partner,” notes Stefano Vignola, Security project manager at Eurotel. “This positioning is not only a recognition of our experience and collaboration but also brings direct operational benefits to customers.”

The installation was completed in just two weeks. The system includes a diverse mix of Axis network cameras tailored to the unique characteristics of each monitored area.

### Network cameras for indoor spaces

* [Dome cameras](https://www.axis.com/products/dome-cameras) were installed for general interior coverage of the coworking spaces.
* [Panoramic cameras](https://www.axis.com/products/panoramic-cameras) were deployed in stairways and corridors at the office. These cameras offer 360° IR illumination and edge-based deep learning analytics.

### Network cameras for outdoor and large areas

* To ensure perimeter protection of the garden area, several dual-sensor, multidirectional panoramic cameras were deployed. These cameras provide excellent image quality even in challenging lighting conditions.
* For parking areas and the sports field, a multidirectional panoramic camera with four channels (5 MP per channel at 20 fps) was installed. This camera provides a panoramic view that eliminates blind spots and can capture both wide-angle and zoomed-in, detailed views.

To ensure perfect aesthetic integration with the building’s refined architecture, all outdoor cameras were custom‑repainted using the customer’s RAL color specifications—making the devices nearly invisible within the environment.

![ Modern black two-story building on stilts with colorful stepping stones on a grassy slope.](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2026-03/best%20remote%20management%20system.jpg.webp?h=60d1d838&itok=ffUIsxDn)

## Maximum scalability with remote management system

The solution features centralized, fully remote management, allowing for seamless control and monitoring from anywhere. With high-quality imaging, it enables accurate event reconstruction, providing clear and detailed visuals.

The system is designed for maximum scalability, making it easy to expand and adapt for future needs, all while leveraging advanced remote management capabilities.

The project delivered four key advantages for Infinite Area:

* Rapid deployment
* Centralized, fully remote management
* High‑quality imaging for accurate event reconstruction
* Maximum scalability for future expansion

## Cloud-based surveillance for sustainable hotel

The scalability and flexibility of this security solution was quickly validated through its adoption in another Infinite Group initiative: The Castelletto, an eco‑sustainable boutique hotel located in Montebelluna’s historic center. The owners sought the same cloud‑based and on-site server free approach to preserve the elegance of the recently restored interiors.

Axis products and solutions now secure the hotel’s common areas, lobby, and surrounding garden —again without the need for local recording devices.

“Recognizing people, identifying those we already know, and sending personalized messages as they enter to create a ‘people‑friendly’ environment is our next challenge,” adds Bof.

"We chose Axis cameras precisely because they allow us to turn this vision into reality."

Patrizio Bof

President and founder of Infinite Group

## Cloud video surveillance for a smarter, safer future

The success of both deployments has opened the door to a new phase: a complete modernization of the video surveillance system at Infinite Area’s headquarters. The upcoming upgrade will follow the same technological philosophy, aiming to standardize security management under a single intelligent interface and eliminate the limitations of legacy systems.

The goal is clear: to transition from traditional surveillance to a proactive protection ecosystem, capable of interacting with the people who live and work within Infinite Area’s spaces and ready to support the challenges of tomorrow.

The solutions implemented at Infinite Area’s locations demonstrate how intelligent security can become a strategic enabler for organizations committed to innovation, digital transformation, and people‑centric design.
