# Creating intelligent perimeter protection for Vergnano

Organization:
F.lli Vergnano

Location:
Chieri (TO), Italy

Customer need:
Intrusion Protection,
Perimeter and area security,
Operational efficiency,
Security,
Safety

Chieri (TO), Italy,
2026

The historic company specializing in the production of high-precision cutting tools relies on intelligent perimeter protection from Axis. The advanced end-to-end video surveillance solution integrates radar, video, and audio and prevents intrusions before they occur, ensuring operational continuity at the Chieri production center.

---

## Vergnano, eighty years of excellence

With nearly eighty years of experience in the high-precision cutting tool industry, Vergnano is a historic company in the sector, specializing in the production of thread taps, threaders, milling cutters, and dies.

Internationally recognized for its product quality and continuous innovation, Vergnano operates in more than 30 countries worldwide. The company designs and manufactures a comprehensive range of precision tapping, gear-cutting, and milling solutions for highly specialized industries, including fasteners, energy, mechanical engineering, electrical, aerospace, and automotive.

Every stage of tool manufacturing is carried out at the heart of Vergnano, at its production facility in Chieri (Turin), where the watchwords are: cutting-edge technology, highly specialized personnel, and strict quality controls.

![Vergnano top view 3](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2025-11/DJI_0178.jpg.webp?h=71976bb4&itok=NDRy4IP4)

## From burglary detection to perimeter protection

Vergnano's production plant, a modern facility covering over 15,000 square meters built in 2002, suffered an average of two to three theft attempts per year. With tools that can be worth thousands of euros and given heavy penalties for failure to deliver to customers, security has always been a pillar of the company's operational continuity.

Vergnano therefore decided to move from a burglary detection approach to a prevention approach, so that threats could be detect and deterred before they could materialize. In other words, the need was to “do something more, but do it outside,” i.e., protect the entire perimeter of the plant in an intelligent and effective way.

"In our industry, security is not just a matter of theft prevention, but is essential to ensuring business continuity and keeping the promises we make to our customers. A production stoppage due to theft has serious economic and reputational consequences. This is where the comprehensive solution offered by Axis comes into play, providing us with effective and reliable proactive protection of our assets, our people, and our reputation."

Sergio Vergnano, CEO

Vergnano

![Fusion camera Axis Vergnano](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2025-11/DJI_0207.jpg.webp?h=7aca9995&itok=U54xzfcn)

## 3 key components in the perimeter protection

The innovative end-to-end solution based entirely on Axis technology was proposed to Vergnano by Sicurtel, a long-standing supplier to the Chieri-based company. Sicurtel has been an Axis Gold Partner since 2004 and is one of Northern Italy's leading distributors of anti-theft, fire protection, video surveillance, access control, home automation, and accessory security systems.
In particular, the choice of Axis was dictated by the unique features of the solution developed, which combines:

* Network cameras and integrated radar-video technology
* Intelligent audio system
* Video management software (VMS)

"At Sicurtel, we had the opportunity to test the fusion solution from Axis, which combines radar and optical technology, from its beta phase, and we immediately recognized its high performance and potential for high-security applications. Integrating radar, video, and audio into a single device that can be installed with a single cable, as Axis has done, is truly revolutionary. We immediately realized that it was the ideal solution for Vergnano's needs."

Claudio Toaldo

Director of Sicurtel

## The solution implemented at the Chieri production plant

360° security: operational advantages that make a difference.

### Radar-video fusion cameras

16 Axis radar-video fusion cameras, which integrate video and radar analysis in [AXIS Object Analytics](https://www.axis.com/products/axis-object-analytics) into a single camera body. The optical part provides immediate visual verification, returning precise localization and classification of objects powered by deep learning. The radar detects the movement, direction, and size of objects in a large area, operating flawlessly in all weather conditions (such as fog or heavy rain) and lighting conditions. Only when both technologies agree on detecting an anomaly does the alarm trigger, drastically reducing false positives.

### Network horn speakers

4 Axis [network horn speakers](https://www.axis.com/products/network-speakers), which go beyond the concept of a simple siren. In the event of unwanted activity, the horn speakers emit pre-recorded messages or allow the security center to communicate remotely in real time with intruders, a highly effective deterrent.

### Bullet cameras

5 Axis bullet optical cameras, which guarantee excellent image quality regardless of lighting conditions.

### Video management software

A single video management software (VMS), AXIS Camera Station Pro, which controls the entire system. A server-based VMS with optional cloud connectivity, creating a complete end-to-end solution managed by a single point of contact.

![Vergnano ACS](/sites/axis/files/styles/3_2_800_x_533/public/2025-11/ALR04023.jpg.webp?h=9dd951d5&itok=jNL6YMn-)

“We have been working with Vergnano for decades, and our role is to anticipate threats by evolving the protection of their site,” continues Toaldo. “The solution offered by Axis has allowed us to build a smarter, more robust, and more efficient perimeter protection for Vergnano, capable of actively preventing theft attempts before they happen, as we have already seen in the field.”

## Tangible and measurable results and benefits

* Effective prevention. Since its activation in 2024, the system has already prevented two major theft attempts: the footage shows the intruders forced to flee, completely disoriented by the clear voice messages emitted by the speakers.
* Drastic reduction in false alarms. The Axis solution reduces false positives to about one per month across the entire system, and even when they do occur, video verification is immediate, meaning that security only intervenes in the event of real threats.
* Cybersecurity and NIS2 compliance by design. Axis has embraced the principles of the European NIS2 directive, integrating them into the design phase of its products and AXIS Camera Station Pro. This has provided Vergnano with the guarantee of a solution that is not only physically secure but also compliant with the latest regulatory standards for cybersecurity.

![Vergnano fusion bullet external](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2025-11/DJI_0213.jpg.webp?h=7aca9995&itok=7UuARlpV)

* Reduced TCO (Total Cost of Ownership). The use of a single device, requiring only one network cable for power and data, one IP address, and one software license for video management, reduces installation and maintenance costs and time compared to a solution with separate sensors and cameras, with an estimated overall TCO savings of approximately 30%.
* Simplified management. An end-to-end solution with a single software also means a single point of contact for both pre- and post-sales support, simplifying all system management activities. In addition, the 5-year product warranty is complemented by the advance replacement of any malfunctioning or damaged parts through an Axis Gold partner such as Sicurtel.
* Improved overall site safety. Radar technology is also used to monitor the speed of vehicles moving within the site, reporting when limits are exceeded, resulting in increased overall safety for personnel on foot.

"The Vergnano project is a perfect example of the strength of a complete Axis solution. By combining our radar-video fusion cameras, audio speakers, and AXIS Camera Station software, you can achieve a seamlessly integrated solution from a single supplier. This not only maximizes performance, but also optimizes management and reduces total cost of ownership. Furthermore, collaboration with certified Gold Partners such as Sicurtel is crucial: their in-depth technical expertise ensures that the full potential of our technology is exploited, translating into concrete results for the end customer."

Pierangelo Bertino

Key Account Manager at Axis Communications

![Horn speaker e radar Vergnano](/sites/axis/files/styles/4_3_800_x_600/public/2025-11/ALR04010.jpg.webp?h=8eba1593&itok=YkUjr5j5)

## Perimeter protection for future growth

Vergnano is constantly growing and already has plans to expand the plant by another 6,000 square meters.

Given the success of the current solution, the plan is to replicate the same security architecture in the new area.

This will ensure a consistent, state-of-the-art level of protection throughout the production site and will take advantage of future innovations and new products that Axis will continue to develop to offer increasingly resilient and secure systems and solutions.
